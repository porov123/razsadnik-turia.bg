Variety characteristics

Rimski

A vigorous variety with very large, rounded nuts.

Variety characteristics

Growth
A very vigorous shrub with an upright crown and few suckers.
Nuts
Very large nuts, about 2.08–4.00 g, rounded and wider than they are long, borne singly or in clusters of 2–3.
Kernel
Large. Kernel percentage: about 36.69–47%.
Pollinizer varieties
Bademoviden, Hempelov, Ran Trapezundski and Cosford.
