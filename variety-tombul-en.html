Variety characteristics

Tombul

A productive variety with regular cropping and an aromatic kernel.

Variety characteristics

Growth
A low-vigour shrub with an open, spreading crown and numerous suckers.
Ripening
8–15 August, according to the grower’s description.
Nuts
Small nuts, about 1.46 g, conical to pointed oval, with a thin shell. The elongated kernel fills the shell well and is tender and aromatic. Kernel percentage: about 59%.
Pollinizer varieties
Bademoviden and other suitable varieties.
Characteristics
Requires suitable soil conditions and adequate air humidity.
