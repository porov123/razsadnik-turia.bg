Variety characteristics

Ran Trapezundski

A variety with a thin shell and a high kernel percentage.

Variety characteristics

Growth
A low-vigour shrub with a spreading crown and numerous suckers.
Nuts
Small to medium-sized nuts, about 1.04–1.72 g, rounded oval with an elongated pointed tip, in clusters of 3–9.
Kernel
Kernel percentage: 58–61%. The shell is dark brown, thin and easy to crack.
Pollinizer varieties
Bademoviden, Rimski, Byal Lombardski, Cosford and Hempelov.
